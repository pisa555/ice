# Team 6 Year 2 Winter Decision Tool

A browser-based comparison tool for two Year 2 winter plans in Pork & Garlic Ice Cream Co.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the folder to Vercel. Edit the highlighted fields to update the model.

## Important data note

The supplied Team 6 note confirms Year 1 winter: Premise D, Sh 1,000 market investment and 50,000 sales. The current Year 1 opening position fields are working estimates until Team 6 enters its actual Year 1 autumn closing cash, annual profit, remaining machine life, debt and tax-loss data.
